## LAUNCH-DAY RANKING CHECKLIST (drives the actual chart position)

1. **Build a launch list now** — the "get notified" email link feeds it.
   Concentrated day-one installs = ranking velocity.
2. **Ask for ratings in-app** after a successful connection streak (never on
   first launch). Rating count + recency is the biggest ranking factor.
3. **Reply to every early review.** Both stores weight developer responses.
4. **Register in Google Search Console** and submit `sitemap.xml`.
5. **Keep the app icon identical** across store + site + favicon (it is now).
6. **Localize** the listing for your top 3 non-US markets once stable.
