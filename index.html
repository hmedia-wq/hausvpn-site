What HausVPN is not

The honest part most VPN sites leave out.

  • It does not make you anonymous or untraceable.
  • It does not hide your activity from services you sign in to.
  • It is not a substitute for good security habits, updates, or two-factor authentication.
  • It is not a shield for illegal activity, and VPN use is restricted in some countries — check your local law.
