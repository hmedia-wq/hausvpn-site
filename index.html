What it actually does

No countdown timers. No fake threat counters. Four things, done properly.

Strict no-logs

No browsing history, no DNS records, no connection timestamps, no source IP. We cannot hand over what we never hold.

WireGuard speed

A lean modern protocol. Connects in about a second, stays fast on mobile, and reconnects cleanly as you move between networks.

Kill switch

If the tunnel ever drops, your internet is blocked instead of silently exposed. Auto-connect handles untrusted Wi-Fi for you.

Nothing hidden

A live panel shows protocol, server, IP, session duration and data transferred. You can always see exactly what is happening.

What HausVPN is not

The honest part most VPN sites leave out.

  • It does not make you anonymous or untraceable.
  • It does not hide your activity from services you sign in to.
  • It is not a substitute for good security habits, updates, or two-factor authentication.
  • It is not a shield for illegal activity, and VPN use is restricted in some countries — check your local law.
